A guided tour for leaders

Executive demo walkthrough

Use this house to tell a connected transformation story: start with the business outcome, explore the teams doing the work, and show how agents can help—within a foundation of governance and security.

◎

1 · The purpose

Make agentic AI tangible across the telco. Each room connects a real business function to the people, challenges, outcomes, and demos that matter there.

⌂

2 · How to use the house

Begin in the Executive Copilot boardroom, then select any room. Review the personas and priorities before opening a linked, working demo in the preview.

✦

3 · What the solutions mean

Agents assist with focused work—insight, recommendations, and action—so people can resolve issues faster, serve customers better, and make decisions with more confidence.

⬡

4 · Why the foundation matters

Agent 365 sits beneath every room. It represents the shared management, governance, and security needed to identify and oversee agents as adoption grows.

Recommended flow: boardroom → rooms → foundation
